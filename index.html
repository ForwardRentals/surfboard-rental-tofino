Where to surf

Tofino's beginner-friendly beaches.

Tofino faces straight into the open Pacific, so there's almost always something to ride. Pick the beach to match the swell and your skill.

  • Chesterman BeachLong, sandy and close to town. A classic first-day beach on smaller days.
  • Cox BayCatches lots of swell and is consistent all year. Best for beginners when it's small.
  • Long BeachKilometres of sand in Pacific Rim National Park Reserve. Easy to find your own peak. A park pass is required to park.
  • Wickaninnish BeachThe south end of Long Beach, with beach access by the Kwisitis visitor centre.

Rip currents are common. Check the forecast, surf near others and ask a local or a surf school when in doubt.

FAQ

Before you paddle out.

How much does it cost to rent a surfboard in Tofino?

Soft-tops on Rented Local start around $30 a day and performance hard-tops around $35. A board and wetsuit combo is about $55 a day. Prices change with the season, so check the live listing.

Do I need a wetsuit in Tofino?

Yes. The Pacific off Tofino stays cold year-round. Most people wear a 4/3 in summer and a 5/4 with boots, gloves and a hood in winter.

Which Tofino beach is best for beginners?

Chesterman Beach and Cox Bay are the usual starting points, and Long Beach in Pacific Rim National Park Reserve has plenty of room to spread out. Conditions change daily, so check the surf forecast and surf within your ability.

Should I take a lesson?

If you have never surfed, yes. Tofino has several surf schools, and a lesson on day one makes the rest of your rentals far more fun.

How does booking work?

Pick a board on Rented Local, choose your dates and send a request. Your booking is confirmed by the local owner before you are charged.

I live in Tofino. Can I rent out my boards?

Yes. List your boards, wetsuits or racks on Rented Local and earn from gear that sits in the shed between swells.
